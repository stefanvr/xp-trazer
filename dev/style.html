The palette table, and the one word its typography section decides. Every colour below is imported from src/render/palette.ts, never re-typed, so a swatch can never show a colour the game does not paint with. Dev-only — this page is never built (see dev/style.html's entry in .claude/skills/preview/SKILL.md).

Colour Object Role

Cleared

The word a cleared level shows, at its own size — drawn by the real renderer, since a colour chip has no face, size or tracking to show.

Game over

The run's score, in the game-over colour, drawn where Cleared is — by the real drawGameOver, over the same level.
