# Brainstorm — 2026-09-05

**Draft. Binds nothing.** A record of a conversation, not a decision and not a design. Nothing here
may be cited as authority; the document that owns each answer is the truth once it exists.

**Subject: which overarching goal comes after version one**, given that extracted material from the
C64 original became available. Grouped by candidate rather than by the specification that will
consume it — the conversation was a comparison between three candidates, and splitting it across four
specs would lose the comparison, which is the whole content.

**Four kinds of entry, kept apart deliberately** — *said* by the owner, *measured* by the agent from
the artefacts, *suggested* by the agent and accepted, and *open*. By the next session the first three
read identically unless they are separated here. **Measured** is a category this file adds to the
`brainstorm` skill's three: it is evidence rather than opinion, and it should not be softened into a
suggestion, nor hardened into something the owner said.

**The material is not in this repository.** It sits in `trazer-artifacts/c64-traz` beside the project,
as five packs from successive reverse-engineering passes over the C64 game. Whether any of it is ever
committed here is untouched by this conversation.

---

## The candidates as the owner framed them

### Said

- **Three things are worth doing now, while the app is small.** *"I would say adding sound, animation
  and sizing ( mobile needs to be landscape instead of potrait), would be better now as the app is
  small/simple."*
- **They are probably not equal.** *"But maybe all are not equal."* This turned out to be the
  productive half of the framing: the comparison, not the list.
- **Sound looks simple but feels coupled to animation, and animation is the harder one.** *"Sound
  from the ... SFX_and_WebAudio_pack seems simple, but related to animation which I would say is
  harder (I need to specify what and how these animations are done)."*
- **Sizing was expected to be cheap by way of importing a level.** *"Sizeing would maybe benefit from
  importing the levels from ... TRAZ_second_pass as that would be relative simple, if we stick to the
  current rules and elements available, as this fixes the real sizing of levels. Maybe even scope it
  down to just 1 trivial level to import."*
- **The order after these two is already held.** Arcade mode including hazards and lives, with levels
  as just a list · extending in level elements and bonuses · introducing the map · introducing
  journey mode. Stated as a sequence, not as a backlog to be recorded anywhere.

## Sound

### Measured by the agent

The pack holds ten effects, each as WAV, OGG, MP3 and Opus, plus a procedural Web Audio recreation
(`web/soundboard.html`) and the parameter model both were generated from (`sounds.json` — per
segment: waveform, frequency sweep, gain, ADSR, low-pass, vibrato).

Against the events `spec-domain.md` actually names:

| Effect | An event version one has? |
|---|---|
| `ball_bounce` | Yes — *Collision* |
| `brick_hit` | Yes — *Element destroyed* |
| `bat_fire`, `bumper_hit`, `gamma_trap`, `alien_spawn`, `alien_destroy`, `power_pill`, `multiball`, `ball_lost` | No — each belongs to a mechanic the domain does not have |

So two of ten apply today. The remaining eight map onto the candidates the owner has already
sequenced behind this one, which is a reason they are worth nothing yet rather than a reason to bring
those candidates forward.

### Suggested by the agent, accepted

- **Sound is not coupled to animation.** They would be triggered by the same thing — something the
  domain announces — but sound needs only that announcement, where animation needs subject matter.
  The owner's *"related to animation"* holds for the trigger and not for the work.
- **The real content of a sound goal is that seam.** `step()` returns state and nothing else today,
  so the edge infers what happened by diffing a collision counter. Sound is the first thing that
  needs the domain to *say* what happened. **A-1** is what makes that non-trivial and also what makes
  it worth having: an event stream is testable over plain state, where an audio assertion is not.
  Everything the owner sequenced afterwards — a lost ball, a hazard, a pickup — wants the same seam.

### Said

- **Sound is the next scope, and the procedural Web Audio model is the form it takes.** Asked as an
  either/or against the rendered clips, and answered without qualification. The agent's own
  recommendation had deliberately left this one open as the goal's first decision rather than the
  scope's; the owner settled it early instead.

### Open

- **Which of the two applicable effects, if not both.** Not asked.
- **Whether sound is part of the feel or the last thing added** — carried forward unresolved from
  `2026-09-02`, where the either/or was answered *"yes"*. Choosing to build it now answers *when* and
  says nothing about *which*, so the question survives this conversation intact.
- **What `spec-style.md` gains.** It owns *"look, sound and feel"* by the routing and currently records
  sound only as left open. Nothing was discussed about what a sound section would say — loudness, an
  aesthetic, a rule for what may make noise.
- **Whether the audio artefacts get committed to this repository, and in what form.** The procedural
  choice makes this smaller than it would have been — a parameter model rather than forty media files
  — but it was not discussed.

## Sizing, and importing a level

### Measured by the agent

The owner's estimate rests on *"if we stick to the current rules and elements available"*. Measured
against `rooms_full.json`, no room does.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Rooms using only brick-like objects | **0 of 64** |
| Object kinds across all rooms | horizontal brick 5236 · dimpled solid block 817 · vertical brick 774 · **horizontal trap 586 · monster generator 375 · vertical trap 363 · bumper 141** |
| Lightest room | room 59 — 81 objects, one unsupported kind (22 vertical traps) |
| Rooms where every bat satisfies **DS-1.6** | **24 of 64** — 72 of 141 bats have neither perpendicular side blocked |
| Bat orientations | 30 rooms horizontal-only, 20 vertical-only, 14 both — where the app assumes two live groups |
| Element footprint | C64 objects are 2×1, 1×2, 2×2, 3×3 and 4×3; `spec-domain.md` says an element occupies exactly one cell, and names that as what makes **DS-2.4** exact rather than approximate |
| Ball start | authored per room, against **DS-1.4** drawing it from the seed |
| Grid | 40×25 cells, against version one's 20×15 |

The free-floating bat is the substantial one: in the original the bat sits *inside* the room with the
ball able to pass either side, which is arguably what distinguishes Traz from Arkanoid. **DS-1.6** is
version one's simplification of that, and importing a real room mostly means giving it up.

So importing one room is not a data job. It reaches DS-1.4, DS-1.6, the one-cell element rule and the
bat-group model, and needs at least one new element kind — which is the *extending in level elements*
goal the owner had already sequenced two places later, arriving early under a different name.

### Suggested by the agent, accepted

- **The owner's instinct was right and only the price was wrong.** Deciding a landscape layout at
  20×15 and then meeting 40×25 later means deciding it twice, so wanting the real proportions first
  is sound.
- **The cheap way to get them is to author a level by hand at the real dimensions**, using a C64 room
  as reference, rather than importing one. That takes the proportions and the landscape pressure
  without touching the domain, and leaves the import where the owner already put it.
- **Level dimensions and layout are one goal, not two.** `spec-app.md` derives its 700px small-screen
  threshold from the level being 640px wide, so the two always change together.

### Open

- **Whether sizing or sound came first was put to the owner and answered by choosing sound**, so the
  ordering question is settled for now and the reshaping above is untested.
- **What "landscape" means concretely** — an orientation lock, a prompt to rotate, or merely a layout
  that suits a wide screen. Not discussed.
- **Whether the original room data ever enters the tree.** Deferred with the import.

## Animation

### Measured by the agent

Two packs hold animation material, and neither has a subject in version one:

| Pack | Contents | Mechanic it belongs to |
|---|---|---|
| `TRAZ_C64_fourth_pass_animations_v2` | eight alien classes with frame tables, a destruction sequence, the power-pill cycle | monster generators, pickups |
| `TRAZ_C64_bat_firing_contraction` | horizontal and vertical bat recoil families, atlas and manifest | bats that fire |

### Suggested by the agent, accepted

- **Animation is not the hardest of the three; it is the one that cannot be specified yet.** There is
  nothing in the domain to animate, so the owner's *"I need to specify what and how these animations
  are done"* has no subject matter to be about.
- **The artefacts would reopen a tension `spec-style.md` deliberately parked** — it commits to Tron
  and records the C64 heritage as flavour that yields wherever the two disagree. C64 sprite frames are
  the disagreement.

### Open

- **What version one could animate if it wanted to** — a brick going, a launch, the arrival of
  `CLEARED`. Named by the agent in passing and not discussed.

## Tensions

### Left standing

- **The Tron commitment against the material now available.** `spec-style.md` chose Tron and treated
  C64 as flavour, at a moment when there was no C64 material to hand. There now is: sprite atlases,
  charsets, sixteen background patterns, eight palette pairs. Nothing forces the question yet, because
  the chosen goal is sound and sound has no look — but the reason the tension was parked has changed,
  and that is worth knowing before the next visual goal rather than during it.
- **Sound arriving before anything to hear it in.** Two effects on a single level is a thin thing to
  build for, and the argument for building it is largely that it opens a seam. **O-1** licenses that
  — the routine is the product — but it is the override doing the work, not the game.

## What a scope cannot yet be written from

- **What done means for a sound goal.** No edge was discussed. Two effects audible is one candidate;
  an event stream the domain owns and the suite asserts is another, and they are not the same size.
- **How sound is verified.** *The suite is the verification* is the standing rule, and what an
  automated check can assert about audio was not discussed. This is the goal's first real problem and
  nothing in this conversation touches it.
- **Where the Web Audio decision is recorded.** The owner has chosen it, and `spec-tech.md` owns
  technology choices — but a choice recorded before the goal that proves it inverts *the choice
  becomes the goal in front of it*.
